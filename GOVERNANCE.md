# Rust Indonesia Governance

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the governance structure, organizational responsibilities, and decision-making processes of Rust Indonesia.

Its purpose is to ensure that the community is managed transparently, responsibly, and sustainably.

## 2. Governance Principles

Rust Indonesia governance is based on the following principles:

- **Transparency:** Community decisions and important activities should be communicated openly.
- **Accountability:** Organizers are responsible for their assigned duties and decisions.
- **Inclusivity:** Community members should have opportunities to participate and contribute.
- **Collaboration:** Decisions should consider community feedback and encourage discussion.
- **Sustainability:** Governance should support the long-term development of the community.
- **Independence:** Rust Indonesia operates as an independent community initiative.

## 3. Organizational Structure

Rust Indonesia uses a lightweight, community-driven organizational structure.

### 3.1 Community Organizers

Community Organizers coordinate the overall operation and development of Rust Indonesia.

Responsibilities include:

- Maintaining the community's direction and objectives.
- Coordinating community activities and events.
- Managing official communication channels.
- Supporting contributors and volunteers.
- Maintaining community documentation.
- Ensuring that community policies are followed.
- Representing Rust Indonesia in external community discussions when appropriate.

### 3.2 Working Groups

Working Groups may be established to support specific areas of community activity.

Possible Working Groups include:

- **Events:** Organizing meetups, workshops, and conferences.
- **Education:** Developing learning materials and educational programs.
- **Community Relations:** Connecting with other Rust communities and organizations.
- **Communications:** Managing announcements, social media, and community updates.
- **Open Source:** Supporting collaborative projects and contributions.

Working Groups may be created, changed, or discontinued according to community needs.

### 3.3 Contributors and Volunteers

Contributors and volunteers help the community through activities such as:

- Organizing events.
- Preparing learning materials.
- Giving technical talks.
- Mentoring beginners.
- Maintaining community projects.
- Supporting communication and outreach.

Participation is voluntary and does not automatically grant governance authority.

## 4. Decision-Making

Rust Indonesia aims to make decisions through open discussion and consensus whenever practical.

The decision-making process should follow these guidelines:

1. Identify the issue or proposal.
2. Share relevant context and objectives.
3. Invite feedback from affected community members and organizers.
4. Discuss alternatives and potential consequences.
5. Reach a decision through consensus whenever possible.
6. Document significant decisions when appropriate.
7. Communicate the outcome to relevant community members.

For urgent operational matters, designated organizers may make temporary decisions and communicate the reasons afterward.

## 5. Community Membership

Rust Indonesia welcomes anyone interested in the Rust programming language.

Community participation is open and does not require formal membership registration.

Members may participate in discussions, events, learning activities, and community projects.

## 6. Organizer Responsibilities

Organizers are expected to:

- Act in the best interests of the community.
- Communicate respectfully and transparently.
- Avoid abusing organizational permissions or authority.
- Disclose potential conflicts of interest.
- Respect the Code of Conduct.
- Protect private information entrusted to them.
- Support a welcoming and inclusive environment.

## 7. Organizer Selection and Changes

Organizer roles may be established as the community grows.

New organizers may be appointed based on demonstrated contributions, willingness to serve, reliability, and community needs.

Changes to organizer responsibilities should be communicated clearly.

The community should avoid relying on a single individual for critical operations whenever practical.

## 8. Conflict of Interest

Organizers and contributors should disclose situations in which personal, professional, or financial interests could influence community decisions.

A person with a significant conflict of interest should not independently make decisions on matters directly affecting that interest.

## 9. Transparency and Documentation

Important governance decisions, policies, and organizational changes should be documented in the community repository.

Public documentation should be preferred unless privacy, safety, or security considerations require information to remain confidential.

## 10. Policy Changes

Governance policies may be updated as Rust Indonesia develops.

Proposed changes should be discussed with relevant organizers and community members.

Significant changes should include a clear explanation and be documented through version-controlled updates.

## 11. Independence and External Relations

Rust Indonesia is an independent local community initiative.

The community may collaborate with the Rust Project, Rust Foundation, other Rust User Groups, educational institutions, companies, and open-source organizations.

Such collaboration does not imply official affiliation, representation, or endorsement unless explicitly confirmed by the relevant parties.

## 12. Governance Review

This document should be reviewed periodically to ensure that it remains relevant to the community's size, activities, and needs.
