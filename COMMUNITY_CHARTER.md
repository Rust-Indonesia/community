Rust Indonesia Community Charter

1. About

Rust Indonesia is an independent community initiative that brings together Rust developers, learners, educators, students, and open-source enthusiasts across Indonesia.

The community aims to provide an inclusive environment where people can learn, share knowledge, collaborate, and contribute to the Rust ecosystem.

2. Vision

To build a connected, inclusive, and sustainable Rust community across Indonesia that actively participates in the global Rust ecosystem.

3. Mission

Rust Indonesia is committed to:

- Making the Rust programming language more accessible to people in Indonesia.
- Supporting learning and knowledge sharing at all experience levels.
- Organizing meetups, workshops, discussions, and community events.
- Encouraging open-source contributions and collaborative projects.
- Connecting Rust enthusiasts, developers, educators, and organizations.
- Building relationships with Rust communities and initiatives worldwide.

4. Community Principles

Our community is guided by the following principles:

- Inclusivity: Everyone is welcome regardless of experience, background, or location.
- Respect: Members should treat one another with respect and professionalism.
- Openness: Knowledge sharing and collaboration are encouraged.
- Learning: Mistakes are part of the learning process.
- Community-driven: Activities and initiatives should benefit the community.
- Transparency: Community decisions and activities should be communicated clearly.

5. Activities

Rust Indonesia may organize and support activities including:

- Local meetups and community gatherings.
- Rust workshops and beginner-friendly learning sessions.
- Technical talks and discussions.
- Open-source projects and collaborative initiatives.
- Online learning activities.
- Participation in international Rust community events.

6. Participation

Anyone interested in the Rust programming language may participate in Rust Indonesia.

Participation is voluntary and does not require professional Rust experience.

Members are encouraged to contribute through learning, sharing knowledge, organizing activities, mentoring, and open-source collaboration.

7. Organization and Governance

Rust Indonesia is a community-driven initiative.

Community organizers are responsible for coordinating activities, maintaining communication channels, supporting members, and ensuring that community guidelines are followed.

Governance procedures and organizer responsibilities are described in separate community governance documents.

8. Code of Conduct

All community activities and communication channels should maintain a welcoming, respectful, and safe environment.

Rust Indonesia adopts the principles of the "Rust Code of Conduct" (https://rust-lang.org/policies/code-of-conduct/).

Local reporting and moderation procedures will be documented separately.

9. Independence and Affiliation

Rust Indonesia is an independent local community initiative.

It is not an official representative of, or endorsed by, the Rust Project or Rust Foundation unless explicitly confirmed by the relevant organizations.

The community respects the Rust trademark policies and applicable guidelines.

10. Charter Maintenance

This charter may be reviewed and updated as the community develops.

Changes should be communicated transparently to the community.
